# 01. Project Overview — JuiceFlow Factory Manager

## 1. Executive Summary
**JuiceFlow Factory Manager** is an enterprise-grade, offline-first mobile ERP system engineered specifically for juice manufacturing businesses, fruit processing plants, and beverage packaging factories.

In traditional beverage and agro-processing businesses across South India and regional processing clusters (such as Theni, Dharmapuri, Salem, and Dindigul), production logging, ingredient ratios, batch quality records, inventory movements, and customer billing are frequently recorded manually on paper ledgers. This leads to:
- Lack of batch traceability during quality issues.
- Inaccurate wastage calculations and fruit spoilage.
- Delayed billing and uncollected customer credit balances.
- Inability to monitor plant productivity in real-time.

**JuiceFlow** transforms factory operations with an intuitive, bilingual (English & தமிழ்) mobile application accessible even to non-technical floor operators, while delivering high-level analytics to factory owners and executives.

---

## 2. Core Value Propositions
1. **End-to-End Traceability:** Complete linkage from raw fruit lot purchases to blending batches, quality lab checks, packaging, and customer sales invoices.
2. **Grassroots-Friendly Design:** Clear, touch-friendly UI designed for factory floor usability in English and Tamil.
3. **Food Safety & Compliance:** Built-in quality inspection capturing Brix sugar levels, pH readings, temperature, organoleptic attributes, and FSSAI/GST details.
4. **Offline Resilience:** SQLite database ensures factory operations never halt during broadband outages.
5. **Instant Invoicing:** Instant PDF Tax Invoices generated with factory branding, GST breakdown, and thermal/A4 printing support.
